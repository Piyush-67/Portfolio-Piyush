About Me

Who I am & what I build

I'm Piyush Kumar Verma, a Software Developer who builds modern, responsive websites and practical digital solutions for real businesses. My work sits at the intersection of clean interface design and functional development — I care as much about how a site feels as how it performs.

My journey into development started with curiosity about how websites work, and grew into a focus on building complete, production-ready projects: business sites, educational platforms, restaurant and hospitality websites, and web applications. Each project taught me something new about translating a real requirement into working software.

My approach is simple: understand the business first, then the audience, then the technology. I build with semantic HTML, maintainable CSS and vanilla JavaScript, and I keep performance, accessibility and responsiveness in mind from the first line of code.

My approach

Business-first development

I believe good software starts with a clear understanding of the problem. Before writing code, I map out the goals, the audience and the constraints — then choose the simplest technology that solves the problem well.

I focus on responsive layouts, clear information architecture and reliable functionality, so the final result is something people can actually use — not just something that looks good in a mockup.
